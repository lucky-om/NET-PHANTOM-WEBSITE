✓ 100% LOCAL PRIVACY ARCHITECTURE

Local Volatile Storage & Packet Telemetry

NetPhantom's core engine is engineered to respect total local data privacy:

  • Volatile Memory Only: Captured frames and dissected protocol trees exist exclusively in volatile system RAM and are wiped upon process termination.
  • Zero Telemetry: No backend analytics, tracking pingbacks, or crash report transmitters exist in the codebase.
  • No Default Exfiltration: By default, no packet logs or host IP addresses are ever transmitted to Luckyverse or third parties.
👻 PHANTOM AI DATA TRANSMISSION

Phantom AI & Third-Party LLM APIs

When utilizing the optional Phantom AI Sandbox or chat assistant, please be aware of the following:

  • Third-Party Processing: Queries, packet metadata, and any information pasted into the AI tools are transmitted over an encrypted TLS connection to the Phantom AI backend for inference.
  • Data Minimization: You should carefully sanitize packet payloads. Never submit raw packets containing PII, plaintext passwords, or sensitive enterprise data to the AI Sandbox.
  • Server-Side Key Storage: API keys are stored securely on a Cloudflare Worker proxy and are never exposed to the browser or our repository.
